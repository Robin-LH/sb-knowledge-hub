# Account & Settings

Manage your personal account profile, password, and security preferences.

## Profile Details
To update your name, profile photo, or email address, navigate to **Account Settings** (accessible by clicking your avatar in the top-right corner of the application).

## Security & Password Resets
We recommend setting a strong password and enabling two-factor authentication (2FA):

* **Reset Password:** If you log in via email/password, you can change your password under **Account Settings → Security**.
* **Two-Factor Authentication:** We support authenticator apps (TOTP). Scan the QR code in the Security tab to secure your account.

*Note: If you signed up using Google or GitHub OAuth, password resets must be managed through those providers.*

## Notification Preferences
Configure how and when SiteBeacon alerts you:
* **Scan Summaries:** Receive an email summary as soon as a scheduled scan finishes.
* **Score Decreases:** Get immediate alerts if your accessibility or performance score drops below a specific threshold.
* **Monthly Digest:** A monthly summary showing website health trends across your workspace.

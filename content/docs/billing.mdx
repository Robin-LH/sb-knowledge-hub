# Billing & Plans

SiteBeacon offers simple, flexible subscription tiers to match your monitoring requirements.

## Subscription Tiers
We offer three main plans:

* **Starter (Free):** Up to 3 projects, 100 pages scanned per month, weekly scans.
* **Pro:** Unlimited projects, 5,000 pages scanned per month, daily/weekly/monthly schedules, team invites.
* **Enterprise:** Custom page limits, dedicated server crawlers, SLA support, white-label PDF reports.

## Managing Your Subscription
To view or update your current plan:
1. Open the workspace switcher and select your workspace.
2. Go to **Workspace Settings → Billing**.
3. Here you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription.

All premium plans are billed on a recurring monthly or annual basis.

## Payment Methods & Invoices
We accept all major credit cards via Stripe. Invoices are generated automatically at the start of each billing cycle and are downloadable directly from the **Billing** settings tab under **Billing History**.

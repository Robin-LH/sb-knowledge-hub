# Understanding Your Carbon Score

Your carbon score estimates the environmental impact of your website. Every time someone visits a web page, data is transferred, servers process requests, and devices render content — all of which consume energy and produce carbon emissions.

## How the Score Is Calculated

SiteBeacon estimates your website's carbon footprint per page view by analyzing several factors:

### Data Transfer

The total amount of data (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images, fonts, video) transferred to load the page. Less data means less energy consumed in networks and devices.

### Server Energy

An estimate of the energy consumed by your hosting infrastructure to serve the page. Websites hosted on green energy providers score higher.

### Client-Side Processing

The energy consumed by the visitor's device to render and process the page. Complex JavaScript and heavy rendering consume more energy.

## Score Ranges

| Range | Rating | Estimated CO₂ per view |
|-------|--------|----------------------|
| 90–100 | Very Clean | Under 0.2g CO₂ |
| 70–89 | Clean | 0.2–0.5g CO₂ |
| 50–69 | Average | 0.5–1.0g CO₂ |
| 0–49 | Heavy | Over 1.0g CO₂ |

To put this in context: the average web page produces approximately 0.5g of CO₂ per page view. A website with 100,000 monthly page views at 0.5g per view would generate approximately 50kg of CO₂ per month.

## Factors That Increase Carbon

- **Large images** — Unoptimized images are typically the largest contributor to page weight
- **Heavy JavaScript** — Third-party scripts, analytics, and client-side rendering frameworks
- **Custom fonts** — Multiple font files and weights add to data transfer
- **Video and media** — Auto-playing or embedded videos significantly increase energy consumption
- **Non-green hosting** — Servers powered by fossil fuel energy sources

## Improving Your Score

The most effective ways to reduce your website's carbon footprint:

1. **Optimize images** — Use modern formats (WebP, AVIF), appropriate dimensions, and lazy loading
2. **Minimize JavaScript** — Remove unused code, defer non-critical scripts
3. **Use system fonts** — Or limit custom fonts to one or two weights
4. **Enable caching** — Reduce repeat data transfers for returning visitors
5. **Choose green hosting** — Select a hosting provider powered by renewable energy

## Step 1: Create Your Account djfhjd

Visit [sitebeacon.io](https://sitebeacon.io) and sign up for an account. You can sign up using your email address or through Google or GitHub authentication.

After creating your account, you will be taken to your dashboard — the central hub for all your website monitoring.

## Step 2: Create a Project

Projects are how SiteBeacon organizes the websites you monitor.

1. Click **New Project** from your dashboard
2. Enter a name for your project (e.g., "Company Website")
3. Add the URL of the website you want to monitor
4. Choose your scan frequency (daily, weekly, or monthly)
5. Click **Create Project**

Each project can monitor one website. If you have multiple websites, create a separate project for each.

## Step 3: Run Your First Scan

Once your project is created, SiteBeacon will automatically queue your first scan. You can also trigger a manual scan at any time by clicking **Scan Now** on the project page.

A scan typically takes between 30 seconds and a few minutes, depending on the size and complexity of your website.

## Step 4: Review Your Results

After the scan completes, you will see three scores on your project dashboard:

| Score             | What It Measures                                            |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Accessibility** | WCAG compliance, semantic HTML, ARIA usage, color contrast  |
| **Performance**   | Core Web Vitals, page load time, resource optimization      |
| **Carbon**        | Page weight, server efficiency, estimated CO₂ per page view |

Click on any score to see a detailed breakdown of the issues found and recommendations for improvement.

## Step 5: Invite Your Team

SiteBeacon supports team collaboration through workspaces. To invite teammates:

1. Go to **Settings → Team**
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter their email address and choose a role (Admin, Editor, or Viewer)

Team members will receive an email invitation to join your workspace.

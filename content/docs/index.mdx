## Welcome

SiteBeacon helps you monitor, measure, and improve your website across three critical dimensions:

* **Accessibility** — Ensure your website meets WCAG guidelines and is usable by everyone
* **Performance** — Track Core Web Vitals and page load metrics to deliver fast experiences
* **Carbon** — Measure your website's digital carbon footprint and work toward sustainability

## What You Can Do

### Monitor Multiple Websites

Add any number of websites to your SiteBeacon dashboard. Each website is scanned automatically on a regular schedule, giving you up-to-date insights without any manual effort.

### Understand Your Scores

SiteBeacon provides clear, actionable scores for each of the three pillars. Each score is calculated based on industry standards and best practices, so you always know where you stand.

### Track Progress Over Time

Every scan is recorded, allowing you to see historical trends and measure the impact of changes you make to your website. Identify regressions early and celebrate improvements.

### Share Reports

Generate and export detailed reports to share with your team, stakeholders, or clients. Reports include score breakdowns, issue details, and recommended actions. Updated

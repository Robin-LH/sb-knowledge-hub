# Reports

Every time SiteBeacon audits a project, it generates a comprehensive diagnostic report. These reports help you find, understand, and resolve issues across your site.

## Reviewing Scan History
Every project dashboard lists previous scans in chronological order under the **History** tab. You can click on any scan record to view the specific report snapshot for that date. This helps you track improvements and catch regressions.

## Exporting Reports
SiteBeacon offers two main export formats:

### 1. PDF Summaries
Ideal for sharing with clients, managers, or stakeholders. The PDF contains:
* High-level accessibility, performance, and carbon scores.
* Historical progress charts.
* A summary checklist of unresolved issues.

To download: click **Export Report → Download PDF** on any scan page.

### 2. Detailed CSV Tables
Best for developers who want to process issues programmatically or import tasks into project managers (like Jira or Trello). The CSV lists:
* The URL of each scanned page.
* The issue description, priority, category, and exact location.

To download: click **Export Report → Download CSV** on any scan page.

# Account & Billing Questions

Manage your SiteBeacon plan, billing preferences, workspaces, and team memberships.

---

## How do I invite teammates?

SiteBeacon allows you to collaborate using shared workspaces:

1. Log in to your SiteBeacon account.
2. Select the workspace from the top-left dropdown.
3. Go to **Workspace Settings → Members**.
4. Click **Invite Member**.
5. Enter your colleague's email address and choose their role:
   * **Admin:** Full access to manage projects, billings, and invite members.
   * **Editor:** Can add/remove projects and trigger scans.
   * **Viewer:** Read-only access to view scan history and scores.

---

## How do I export reports?

You can export PDF or CSV reports for any scan:

1. Open the project dashboard.
2. Click on the specific scan you want to export from the **History** tab.
3. Click the **Export Report** button in the top-right corner.
4. Select your preferred format:
   * **PDF Summary:** A beautifully designed executive summary page showing core scores.
   * **Detailed CSV:** A comprehensive spreadsheet showing all pages and discovered issues.

---

## What happens if I exceed my page limit?

Each SiteBeacon plan has a total monthly page scan limit. If a crawl exceeds your remaining limit:

* The crawl will stop once it reaches the limit.
* You will receive a notification email.
* You can upgrade your plan under **Billing Settings** to increase your limit immediately.

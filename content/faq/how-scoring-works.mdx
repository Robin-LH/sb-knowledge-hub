# How Scoring Works

SiteBeacon provides high-level scores out of 100 for accessibility, performance, and carbon. Here is how individual page parameters translate into your project-level ratings.

---

## Page-level vs. Project-level Scores

Each scanned page gets its own specific score for accessibility, performance, and carbon. 

Your **Project Score** is the average of all scanned page scores within that project, weighted equally. For example, if you scan 5 pages with accessibility scores of `90, 95, 80, 85, 100`, the project accessibility score will be **90**.

---

## Why Did My Score Change?

It is common to see scores fluctuate between scans. Here are the most frequent reasons:

### 1. Code Changes
A developer may have deployed updates that fixed or introduced issues (e.g., adding an unoptimized image decreases performance/carbon scores; adding a form without labels decreases the accessibility score).

### 2. Dynamic Content or Third-party Scripts
If your pages load dynamic content (like news feeds, dynamic comments, or external ad scripts), these assets might change and introduce layout shifts or load heavier resources.

### 3. Server Response Speeds
Performance scoring is partially dependent on server latency. If your hosting server is experiencing high load or slow network times during a scan, your performance score may decrease slightly.

### 4. Page Discoverability
If new pages are added to your site, our crawler will discover them. If those new pages have lower scores, they will bring down the project average.
